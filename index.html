A little about us

AR Interior Designing was established in January 2024 with a simple purpose: to create beautiful, functional spaces that feel personal to the people who live in them.

From modular kitchens and custom wardrobes to elegant ceilings and thoughtfully designed living spaces, our team brings together practical planning, refined aesthetics and careful execution.

Founder & CEO Mr. Manikanta ✦ AR Interior Designing ✦Founder & CEO Mr. Manikanta ✦ AR Interior Designing ✦

With a founder-led approach, Mr. Manikanta guides the studio's vision and commitment to thoughtful design, dependable service and quality craftsmanship. We welcome clients at our Beeramguda and Gachibowli locations.
